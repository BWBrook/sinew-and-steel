# Iron & Ruin
### Skin add-on for Hazardry

![](../assets/covers/ss_logo_iron_and_ruin.png){.wrap-right width=2in}

*Grim sword-and-sorcery in the spirit of pulp adventure.*

Use this skin with the Hazardry core rules. The core governs everything else.

Iron & Ruin is for tales of iron and ash: barbarian courage, decadent cities, and sorcery that always exacts a price. Heroes can defy fate, but the gods, the cults, and the old powers keep ledgers.

Suggested creation tone: pulp (12 build points).

## Scroll of Grim Tales (Adventurer-facing rules)

### Attribute labels

| Core slot | Iron & Ruin label | Covers |
|--|---|------|
| Might | **Brawn (BRN)** | raw strength, ferocity, melee power |
| Reflex | **Agility (AGI)** | speed, balance, tumbling |
| Intellect | **Cunning (CUN)** | stealth, guile, precision |
| Empathy | **Will (WIL)** | grit against fear, mental steel, sorcery control |
| Luck | **Fortune (FOR)** | whim of the gods, and the token pool |

### Fortune (Luck)

Tokens are wolf-tooth talismans, spent exactly as Luck tokens.

When the Custodian calls for pure chance, they say: **"Test your Fortune."** Roll under your current tokens.

A rest by a safe hearth restores 1 token, and a milestone refills them; priestly blessings or stolen relics may restore more.

**Rules reminder:** a natural 1 always succeeds and a natural 20 always fails, whatever the target; neither can be nudged.

### Weapons and edge

| Weapon | Edge |
|---|--|
| Fist, cudgel | 0 |
| Broadsword, war-axe, short bow | +1 |
| Greatsword, long bow, sorcerous bolt | +2 |

Fists and cudgels are usually non-lethal: at 0 Stamina the target is out cold, unless you choose otherwise or the fiction demands worse.

### Armour and soak

| Protection | Soak |
|---|--|
| Leather jerkin | 1 |
| Ring mail / hide coat | 2 |
| Steel plate & shield | 3 |

Damage is **1 + edge + 1 per full 5 points of margin - soak**, minimum 1; a natural 1 ignores soak and adds +1.

### Recovery

A draught of wine by the fire restores 1 Stamina after a calm scene; a full bed and ministrations restore 2 instead, up to your maximum. Severe wounds need a healer's skill, potent herbs, or time.

### Equipment and burden

A character may carry up to six substantial items: armour, weapons, bundles, or large tools. More gives Disadvantage on Agility tests. Trinkets and small gear ride in pouches outside that limit.

Coin is an inconvenient necessity; carry too much, and brigands will smell it.

### Sorcery

Steel is trustworthy; spells are treacherous. Any feat is possible in theory, but risk scales brutally.

| Tier | Example intent | Roll | Cost on success | Cost on failure |
|--|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Whisper | Spark a torch, seal a minor wound, chill wine | Test WIL; you may spend Fortune to nudge | None | Fizzles (no Doom) |
| Weave | Hurl a flame bolt, cloud a guard's mind, walk unseen a heartbeat | Test WIL; you may spend Fortune to nudge | Spend 1 Fortune token or lose 1 Stamina | +1 Doom |
| Wrack | Shatter iron gates, summon a shadow hound, freeze a river ford | Test WIL (no Fortune nudges) | +1 Doom, even on success | +2 Doom and a backlash (the Custodian chooses or rolls) |
| Wyrd | Rewrite the memory of a city, call a storm-god's spear | Pay +3 Doom, then test WIL (no Fortune nudges); a natural 1 is legend | The tale echoes for ages | The Custodian's worst, and an immediate backlash |

#### Backlash table (d6)

| d6 | Backlash |
|-|--------|
|1| Flesh withers (lose 1 Stamina) |
|2| Possessed by a minor demon for one scene |
|3| Blind for a day; a third eye opens at night (Advantage on sorcery) |
|4| Doom spills over: mark +1 more Doom |
|5| Eldritch mark: foes sense you; Disadvantage on stealth until purged |
|6| Roll twice and apply both. |

#### Casting steps

![](../assets/art/ss_iron_ruin_sorcery_tiers.png){.margin-right width=1.5in}

1. Declare the effect and its tier.
2. Note the current Doom: its step penalties apply to this casting.
3. Pay any upfront cost (Wyrd's +3 Doom), then test WIL. You may nudge a Whisper or a Weave with Fortune, never a Wrack or a Wyrd.
4. Apply the tier's cost for success or failure, including any backlash, and narrate the fallout.
5. If the casting took Doom to 5 or more, resolve one crisis after the action, then reset Doom to 0 with nothing carried over. The tier's backlash still applies separately.

**Rules of thumb**

- **Whisper:** cantrips; costs nothing but time.
- **Weave:** combat tricks; Fortune or blood can power them.
- **Wrack:** story shakers; even success scars the soul.
- **Wyrd:** campaign-level sorcery; Doom will bite and history will buckle.

A natural 1 ignores soak, as on any attack, and lands with double the narrative punch. A natural 20 fails, as always, and the Custodian may add +1 Doom if it fits the bargain.

_Sorcery is a bargain with things that do not love you._

### Doom track (Pressure)

![](../assets/art/ss_iron_ruin_doom_sigils.png){.margin-left width=1.5in}

Doom is the Pressure track for this skin: the shadow of magic and the debt of the unseen. The sorcery table says exactly when it rises.

| Step | Portent | Custodian twist |
|-|---|-----|
| 0 | Calm | None |
| 1 | Whispers | Cosmetic omens |
| 2 | Blood sigils | Minor Disadvantage |
| 3 | Mind tremor | NPC fear; rituals cost +1 Fortune token |
| 4 | Veil thins | Environment warps; sorcery backlash deals +1 damage |
| 5 **Crisis** | Possession / Withering | Trigger a Doom crisis, then reset Doom to **0** |

Purge Doom through a cleric's rite, a sacrificial pyre, or a grim personal cost.

#### Doom crisis (d6)

|d6| Crisis |
|-|------|
| 1 | Demon grip: lose control for one scene. |
| 2 | Soul scar: lose 1 Stamina; gain an eerie stigma. |
| 3 | Eldritch eruption: area damage. |
| 4 | Mind shatter: Disadvantage on the key roll of the next perilous beat, chosen by the Custodian. |
| 5 | Curse brand: foes scent you across leagues. |
| 6 | Roll twice and stack the results. |

### Heroic Act (once per session)

Declare a cinematic stunt: kick a foe from the battlements, snap a chain, cut a rope bridge at the right moment. Pay 1 Fortune token and gain Advantage on the roll. If you succeed, either add +1 edge to the harm or inflict a simple status: disarmed, stunned, or driven back.

## Chronicler's Touchstone (Custodian-facing rules)

### Voice

![](../assets/art/ss_iron_ruin_idol.png){.wrap-right width=2in}

Use terse, muscular prose: short sentences, vivid verbs, few adjectives. Work through physical sensation: iron, sweat, blood, stone. Violence is sudden. Sorcery is foul and wondrous, as much curse as gift. Civilisation is decadent, but heroes still carve fate with their hands, and their deeds show who they are. Lift the register only for foreboding or grandeur, and use archaic diction sparingly ("eldritch", "abysmal", "thews").

**Frame a beat:**

> The idol's eyes are rubies. The chamber reeks of old incense and newer blood. Somewhere beyond the lintel, a priest begins to chant your name.

### Pacing

Keep rolls sparse. Heroes should triumph more than they fail, yet peril must feel real. Use Advantage to reward cunning gambits and Disadvantage to punish hubris. When in doubt, cut to action, raise a question, or unveil a terrible wonder.

Offer Fortune when failure is within range, and remind players that tokens can also deepen a winning margin to shred armour. Fortune is precious: grant extra recovery rarely, so its last flickers feel desperate. Hand out Doom sparingly, then make its consequences unforgettable.

In this genre, fate collects: scars, debts, curses, and grudges accumulate. Failure changes the world and keeps the story moving. Reserve death for closing an arc or opening a darker one; otherwise, maim, curse, or impoverish.

If it helps you keep track, label scenes by locale (Tomb A1, Bazaar B3).

### Milestones and moves

Milestones come at the usual pace, every 3-4 perilous beats. Let them land on a climactic victory, a perilous journey survived, or a legendary secret unearthed, and make the boons tangible: an enchanted blade, a scar that cows peasants, the trust of a desert tribe.

Reliable Custodian moves:

- cultists advance,
- storm blackens the sky,
- a bridge rots beneath booted feet,
- blood stains the moon,
- a hard bargain is offered,
- coin buys betrayal.
