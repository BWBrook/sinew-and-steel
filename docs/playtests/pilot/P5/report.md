# P5 orchestrator report — Rust & Domes

P5 completed a two-act session at seven beats. Rhea Voss and Jori Vale restored air to six workers, preserved a diverted-cartridge ledger, and placed copies with independent witnesses. A failed confrontation left the buyer and a legally frozen freight bin unresolved. The session closed through the harness, with all 15 public Custodian replies preserved and matched to checkpoints. Fable's cross-team audit is pending; this report describes the run and its limitations rather than certifying every ruling.

Its preferred exclusion of beat 6 from perilous credit remains a judgement call; see `../review-1.md` for both views. The historical records and the original observations below are preserved.

## Procedure and pacing

The log records seven beats, four perilous, split 4/3 across the acts. Two beats contained tests: Jori's Skill repair (G005–G007, events 3–5) and Rhea's opposed Mind confrontation (G011–G013, events 10–13). Both followed declared stakes, a deferred draw, a player Luck decision, and settlement. Jori accepted a success and Rhea accepted a loss against an NPC natural 1; neither spent Luck. The first act ended when the archive was secured, the second when the party returned and placed the evidence with a workers' representative.

One shared tamper alarm raised Heat 0→1 (G009–G010, event 7). Both players independently chose the same action; the Custodian attributed disconnection to Rhea and the export to Jori, charging the common cost once. The milestone at beat 5 followed the third logged perilous beat (events 14–17), gave both characters two points and a boon, and refilled already-full pools. Both banked the points. Jori requested and used his escort favour at departure; its spent status appears in his sheet notes, campaign recap and G014–G015, while the advancement entry retains the original award.

Against Almanac §9's descriptive sketch, P5 was short: 2/7 beats had tests, 4/7 were marked perilous, and 1/7 raised Pressure. There was no fight, rest, Luck test or crisis. These are observations, not targets or balance evidence. The players chose the last tram and a safe evidence handoff; extending that ending to reach ten beats would not improve the trial. The auditor should still judge whether each recorded scene and the departure's perilous flag fit the definitions.

## Friction, coherence and coverage

At G002–G003 both players independently volunteered for the same crawlway job and assigned the other to the shelter. G004 asked Rhea alone to settle the division of labour. No neutral stall prompt was sent. Starting with that relay, completed declarations accompanied the next Custodian reply, so each player could hear the other's public dialogue after both had answered. Earlier relays supplied only the Custodian's summary. Future runs should give all participants the same completed table dialogue from the outset.

The story maintained a clear chain from air failure through suspect cartridges to documentary evidence and its custody. Failure changed the available route without destroying already-secured evidence. The nonviolent resolution and the usable escort boon gave player choices consequences. The opening salvage claim was not explicitly settled in the final reply; retain that as an unresolved reward question for the audit or a continuation.

The players' declarations became highly similar: both photographed the cartridges, requested copies, disconnected the lead, declined the pursuit and chose the workers' representative. Separate agents supplied them independently, but character differentiation was slight. This is a limitation of this table, not evidence that one character or attribute dominates the game. No human fun rating was collected; the narrative had understandable stakes, but repeated coordination and copying decisions slowed it.

Rust-specific coverage was narrow. Technician Advantage and a shared Heat charge occurred. Psionics had possible applications in the frozen scenario, but the Custodian did not explicitly offer an activation and Rhea never attempted one. The frozen M-field sensor clue never appeared in the public transcript. No psi cost or backlash, pure-chance Luck test, Heat step effect, purge, crisis, combat or Deflection was exercised. The G009 export race was offered but bypassed by paying Heat; G014 offered a public leak with a Heat consequence, which the players declined. Do not count a possible fictional opening as a tested procedure.

## Harness and evidence limits

The recorded 83 CLI calls returned successfully; the final campaign validator returned `ok`. The frozen log has 20 consecutive events ending in `session_end`. Gameplay draws use the first two declared seeds, with the results retained through settlement. The 15 comparisons establish that each recorded public reply matched the checkpoint before the next reply replaced it. Individual reply files survive, as do initial and final snapshots, command receipts and the frozen scenario. No engine or rules files changed for this run.

The source packets were assembled from the required public material, but their actual inline delivery was a compact transcription for Rhea and a fuller transcription for Jori, rather than byte-identical delivery of those files. This is a protocol deviation and a context-comparability limitation. The preserved packet files must not be represented as exact records of what each agent received.

Players were instructed to use only supplied text and no tools. Shared filesystem/tool access was not removed, and the bundle has no complete player-tool trace. The public transcript alone cannot establish isolation or absence of private access. The cross-team auditor must assess observed disclosures separately. This run therefore supplies bounded evidence about an instructed-access table, not a secure agent sandbox.

There were no play-time rules corrections, die substitutions, command repairs or stall interventions. The Custodian debrief was requested after closure. That debrief records the author's reasoning but is not independent evidence that its rulings were correct. Invented-rule and leakage verdicts remain for Fable.

## Changes to consider before the programme

- Deliver the complete public packet as literal text, and preserve the exact delivered text consistently for every player.
- Relay completed public dialogue to every player from the start, while continuing to withhold pending independent answers.
- Retain per-reply checkpoint evidence and the command recorder; add actual per-role usage and access traces if the platform exposes them. Do not estimate token costs from reply counts.
- Make signature-procedure opportunities visible when the fiction supplies them, without directing a player to use them. P5 does not replace a psi/backlash test; any prescribed probe should be separately labelled.
- Keep the current rules frozen until the cross-team audit and combined pilot review. No balance change follows from this short, low-Pressure session.
