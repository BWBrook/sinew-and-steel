## What the procedure exercised

The lock test demonstrated a complete public stakes → commitment → deferred roll → player choice → settlement exchange (G007–G009). Pip rolled 17/18 with Advantage against DEX 16 and paid one Fortune to succeed. The Custodian explicitly considered Jack-of-Trades and explained that no other attribute would improve the roll. The record contains one draw, not a reroll. A later warm-hearth rest restored the coin.

All four players received frozen public packets with their drives, owned tags, reviewed gear and named spells. Completed public dialogue was relayed before each new decision, including the intervening single-player lock sequence. The closing public reply was delivered to all four without requesting another action.

That delivery did not eliminate similar or crossed declarations. At G009 three players wanted to investigate the names while Pip wanted to secure the book. At G010 those three adopted Pip's proposal just as Pip agreed to theirs. The Custodian returned the group to the village in G011. Many later replies independently repeated the same caution or question. This is an observed coordination cost, not proof that the drives or relay changes failed generally. The scenario remained coherent and the book/name objectives were resolved, but the low exposure to risk produced little mechanical tension. No separate player enjoyment assessment was collected.

## Coverage and interpretation limits

The frozen scenario allowed a dry route, safe negotiation, and voluntary withdrawal. The players used those options. No one entered the flooded trap route, rang the bell, stood beneath the weak ceiling, fought, cast a spell, invoked a knack, made a Fortune test, or triggered a crisis. Named spells were delivered but never used; their tier payments and backlash were therefore untested. Jack-of-Trades was considered but not activated. No automatic no-choice settlement was exercised because the only roll had a meaningful Fortune choice.

Zero Fatigue here cannot establish that the track is ineffective or that the Custodian omitted a mandatory charge. Careful mapping and talk have explicit fictional resolution in this skin, and the party avoided the scenario's signposted hazards. Whether any actual interval warranted a shared hazard charge remains for the audit. Two logged perilous beats did not reach the printed three-to-four milestone interval; no award was made.
