G002 rolls and settles Tarra's herb search (E004–005) and Grak's tracking (E006–007), then narrates the natural-20 cost and ticks Pack Learns 2→3 (E008). This violates `rules/core/adventurers_manual.md:202-209`, `rules/core/custodians_almanac.md:175-180` and `skills/agent_dm_handbook.md:18-20`. The locked 20 and Tarra's completed success left no material Luck choice at G002; G003 and the calf attack correctly deferred settlement for P003 and P016. The missed pre-roll commitment remains a procedure error despite that mitigation.

**Wrong — beat 7's non-perilous flag; milestone threshold unresolved.** G019/P019 says a failed chest-rot rite spreads illness to a second child; Tarra's stated goal is to stop that spread. G020 records beat 7 as non-perilous (E112). A beat is perilous when failure could cost Stamina, a life **or the goal** (`rules/core/custodians_almanac.md:186-201`; `skills/agent_dm_handbook.md:89-98`). The child's future survival is uncertain, but the containment goal was at immediate risk, which is enough. Beat 3's thin-ice crossing is correctly perilous: the declared failure costs Grak 1 Stamina (G010, E042–044), even though the crossing succeeded. The first P2 award after beat 4 (E082–086, G015) is correct: beats 2, 3 and 4 are the third perilous scene since P1's closing award. With beat 7 corrected, beats 5, 7 and 9 (E099,112,119) are the next three. A second award was **eligible** after beat 9, but the rule permits awarding on the third *or fourth* perilous beat (`rules/core/custodians_almanac.md:121-129`; `skills/agent_dm_handbook.md:96-98`); I found no fixed three-beat cadence in the P2 manifest or notes. Its absence at G023 is therefore not a proven milestone violation. The reported five perilous flags reflect the saved log, while the rule-consistent count is six. Preserve the historical log and make the intended award threshold explicit before the next session.

**Judgement call — no Shadow gains.** The log carries Shadow 1 through all action rolls, records no gain, and purges 1→0 at E120. The two failed risky-rite dice were not final failures: Tarra's child rite became a success after P019's 3-bead nudge (E109–111); the vision test also succeeded (E100–101). The pack fights, G011's screeching drag on open ice, and time under wolf/storm threat offer plausible “noisy desperation” or “time passing under threat” triggers, however. I cannot derive a mandatory number from this fiction. The report's statement that failed rites were the *only* live Shadow source is too narrow; zero gain is a consequential Custodian choice to examine in later runs, not a balance conclusion.

**Correct — final purge, with discretionary timing.** G023/E120 removes one Shadow after the great hunt, feeding rather than fighting the pack, and the clan/Ysre shelter decision. A great hunt and hard-won clan safety are printed purge sources (`skins/clanfire.md:107-109`; `rules/core/custodians_almanac.md:73-76`). It could have been taken earlier, but waiting until the shelter decision completed the safety arc is defensible. No crisis occurred: the party track never reached five (`rules/core/custodians_almanac.md:58-60,80-85`). There is no crisis target or table result to audit.

**Judgement call — Ketha's repeated dart Advantage.** Grak's four spear attacks use the same kind of situational help at E018,035,065,077 (G005, G008, G014, G015). Ketha is fictionally present with a ready dart in the first fight and on the ice; the source permits Advantage when a situation or right tool helps, and gear/context is a Custodian call (`rules/core/adventurers_manual.md:33-40,143-150`; `skills/agent_dm_handbook.md:21-24`). Granting it on four thrusts is generous but not prohibited; no ammo or independent NPC action limit for her is specified. It should be explained each time, as here, rather than treated as a permanent buff. G008 says the fluted wolf “cannot turn its guard” but still rolls normal defence (E035); that sentence overstates the mechanical effect. G014 gives the later flute win its promised effect by recording the wolf's pass (E070).

**Judgement call — two close recoveries.** G018/E106–108 records an act-break hearth pause: Grak 4→6 Stamina with deep shelter/patient care and 4→5 beads; Tarra 3→4 Stamina after buying max 4. G020/E113–115 records a night's sleep and another bead each, Grak 6→7 Stamina. The rite and council talk between dusk and sleep give a possible second pause, but the fiction also reads as one continuous night at the same hearth; the saved record cannot decide this boundary. Mark the second recovery as contingent on treating sleep after the active scene as a new pause, and state that separation more clearly in future play. The earlier G001/E003 dawn recovery, after two elapsed days since P1, is separately plausible.

**Judgement call — vision shard answer exceeds the requested bit.** At P017 Tarra asks whether the pack will come for Grak. G018 answers “no,” then adds that four hurt wolves lie in the den; E100–101 shows a successful 3 against 11 with no Shadow. The printed boon promises one true yes/no answer about the next beat, even on failure (`skins/clanfire.md:142-146,148-159`). Extra detail is not expressly forbidden, but it gives more intelligence than the one-question form promises. Keep future shard answers to the answer plus necessary clarity. The player invoked the boon; the `check` used current Instinct 11 without `--luck-test`, matching the harness distinction between a player-chosen Luck-attribute test and a Custodian-called test (`docs/ai_play_harness.md:249-251`; `tools/play.py:72`).

**Judgement call — adult she-wolf Beast Bond offer.** G021 offers a Spirit test to bond with the wild adult. The skin describes a bonded beast “such as a wolf raised from a pup” and a three-bead pool, but does not define acquisition or require infancy (`skins/clanfire.md:74-78`). Its boon seed specifically grants a wolf *pup* (`skins/clanfire.md:148-159`); that example is not an exclusive route. P021 declines, so no unearned pool or cost entered state. A future rule sentence should say how a bond is earned; this encounter does not prove an illegal acquisition.
