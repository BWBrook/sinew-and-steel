# Clanfire: Emberfall (player handout)

## A player-facing starter brief for Sinew & Steel

Your clan's hearth is dying in a hard winter, and the hunt that should save you is already being claimed by hungry things in the birch-line.

---

## What you know at the start

- The fire is low. If it dies, the cold will get into the cave.
- The clan needs meat and fat before the next hard snow.
- The old hunters say the birch-line feels wrong tonight.

## What to track

- **Instinct beads** (Luck tokens): after a roll, spend beads to nudge the die, 1 point per bead. When the Custodian says "Test your Instinct", roll under the beads you have left; with none, only a natural 1 succeeds.
- **Stamina:** injuries, exhaustion, and how much punishment you can still take. At 0 you collapse.
- **Shadow** (Pressure): rises when taboo, spirits, fear, or hungry attention close in. At 5 a crisis hits, and the track resets to 0.

---

## Fast-start characters

- **Grak of Tall Cliffs (Hunter):** MGT 12 | FLT 10 | CUN 10 | SPR 8 | INS 8/8 | STM 7/7 | Shadow 0/5. Tag: Megafauna tracker (Advantage when tracking big game). Stone spear (+1), hand-axe (+1), hide cloak (soak 1), ochre pouch, sinew cord.
- **Tarra the Ember-Singer (Shaman):** MGT 6 | FLT 8 | CUN 12 | SPR 14 | INS 11/11 | STM 3/3 | Shadow 0/5. Bone flute (Advantage calming beasts), fire-bow drill, herbs, strange cloth.

---

## First beat: what do you do?

The hearth is down to embers. Frost beads on the cave mouth. Outside, beneath the clicking birches, you hear a soft wet crunch: teeth on bone.

Pick an approach, or propose your own:

1. Read the tracks downwind (Cunning).
2. Stalk the sound with spear ready (Fleetness).
3. Call to the spirits for an omen (Spirit; on a miss, +1 Shadow).
4. Wake the clan and bar the cave (no roll; time passes).
